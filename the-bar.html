The Bar

From horse float to cocktail bar

A working horse trailer, stripped back and rebuilt as a matte black bar with warm timber and brass. Built in Palmerston to turn up, look the part and pour properly.

Our story

The conversion

The Trough Bar started life the way most horse trailers do: hauling horses. When we found it, it was tired, dusty and well past its float days, but the bones were good and the shape was perfect.

We stripped it back to the frame, cut in a serving hatch, lined it with timber and finished the outside in matte black. Inside there’s cold storage, a proper bar top and room for our bartenders to work quickly without getting in each other’s way.

The name is a nod to where it came from. A trough is where you water the horses. Ours just pours something better.

Our method

Why pre-batched cocktails

Building every cocktail from scratch looks impressive for the first ten minutes. Then the queue starts. We batch your cocktails ahead of service, then shake, pour and garnish each one to order.

  • Speed. A finished cocktail in your guest’s hand in seconds, not minutes.
  • Consistency. Measured and balanced once, so the last drink of the night tastes like the first.
  • Less queueing. Shorter lines at the bar, more people on the dance floor.

Our people

The team

Every bartender behind The Trough Bar holds a current Responsible Service of Alcohol (RSA) certificate. They’re friendly, quick and well presented, and they know how to keep a bar moving at a busy event.

They also look after your guests. Our team serves responsibly, keeps an eye on the room and can refuse service when it’s the right call.

We arrive around 45 minutes before service to set up and stay around 45 minutes after to pack down. Both are included in every booking.
