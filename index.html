How dry hire works

You supply the alcohol. We bring everything else.

  1. Enquire and lock in your date

    Tell us about your event. We’ll confirm availability, recommend a package and design your signature cocktails.

  2. Shop from our list

    We send a shopping list with exact quantities for your guest count, so you buy what you need and nothing more.

  3. We set up, serve and pack down

    Our RSA-certified bartenders arrive early, batch your cocktails, serve your guests and leave the site as we found it.
