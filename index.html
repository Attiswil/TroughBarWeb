How dry hire works

You supply the alcohol. We bring the bar, the bartenders and everything else.

  1. Send an enquiry

    Tell us the date, venue and guest count. We’ll confirm availability and send a quote.

  2. Buy the alcohol

    We plan the menu with you, then send a shopping list with quantities for your guest count.

  3. We run the bar

    We arrive about 45 minutes before service to set up, serve for your booked hours, then pack down.
