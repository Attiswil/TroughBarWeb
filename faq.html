FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The details on dry hire, bookings, space and travel. If your question isn’t here, just ask.

What is dry hire?

Dry hire means you supply the alcohol and we supply everything needed to serve it: the bar, RSA-certified bartenders, glassware, ice, mixers and garnish, menu signage and your signature cocktails. Setup and packdown are always included.

Do I need a liquor licence?

At dry hire events the host supplies the alcohol and provides it to their guests free of charge – nothing is sold. Our RSA-certified bartenders serve responsibly and can refuse service. Requirements can vary by event and venue, so we’ll talk you through what applies to yours.

Who supplies the alcohol, and how much do I need?

On dry hire, you do. Once your menu is confirmed we send a shopping list with quantities worked out for your guest count and hours of service, so you know exactly what to buy.

Are your bartenders RSA certified?

Yes. Every bartender on our team holds a current Responsible Service of Alcohol (RSA) certificate.

What is the minimum booking?

The Cocktail Bar has a minimum of 4 hours of service. The Dirty Soda Bar is available from 2 hours. Setup and packdown (around 45 minutes each) are on top of your service time and included free.

How much space and power do you need?

We need a flat, firm area of about Xm x Xm with vehicle access to tow the trailer in, and access to a standard 10 amp power outlet. If your venue is tight or off-grid, let us know and we’ll work through the options.

Where do you travel, and are there travel fees?

We’re based in Palmerston NT. Travel is free within 50 km. Beyond 50 km a travel fee of $X/km applies.

How do I book, and is there a deposit?

Send us an enquiry with your date and event details. A deposit of $X secures your booking, with the balance due X days before your event.

Can you do alcohol-free events?

Yes. Our Dirty Soda Bar is completely alcohol-free and suits family events, school functions and corporate days. We can also run it alongside the Cocktail Bar so every guest has something good in hand.

Can you supply the alcohol?

Fully catered service, where we supply the alcohol, is available on request. Enquire for a quote.
