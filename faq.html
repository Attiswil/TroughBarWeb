Straight from the horse’s mouth.

Your questions answered: how dry hire works, what we need on site, and how to book.

What is dry hire?

You supply the alcohol and we supply everything needed to serve it: the bar, RSA-certified bartenders, glassware, ice, mixers and garnish, menu signage and your signature cocktails. Setup and packdown are always included.

Do I need a liquor licence?

At dry hire events the host supplies the alcohol and provides it to their guests free of charge – nothing is sold. Our RSA-certified bartenders serve responsibly and can refuse service. Requirements can vary by event and venue, so we’ll talk you through what applies to yours.

Who supplies the alcohol, and how much do I need?

On dry hire, you do. Once the menu is confirmed we send a shopping list with quantities based on your guest count and hours of service.

Are your bartenders RSA certified?

Yes. Every bartender holds a current Responsible Service of Alcohol (RSA) certificate.

What is the minimum booking?

The Cocktail Bar has a minimum of 4 hours of service. The Dirty Soda Bar is available from 2 hours. Setup and packdown take about 45 minutes each, on top of your service time, and are included free.

How much space and power do you need?

A flat, firm area of about Xm x Xm with vehicle access for towing the trailer in, and a standard 10 amp power outlet. If your venue is tight or has no power, tell us when you enquire.

Where do you travel, and are there travel fees?

We’re based in Palmerston NT. Travel is free within 50 km. Beyond 50 km the fee is $X/km.

How do I book, and is there a deposit?

Send an enquiry with your date and event details. A deposit of $X secures the booking, and the balance is due X days before the event.

Can you do alcohol-free events?

Yes. The Dirty Soda Bar is completely alcohol-free. It can also run alongside the Cocktail Bar.

Can you supply the alcohol?

Fully catered service, where we supply the alcohol, is available on request. Enquire for a quote.
