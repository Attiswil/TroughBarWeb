Good to know

Who brings what

On dry hire, the client supplies the spirits and other alcohol. Once your menu is set, we provide a shopping list with quantities for your guest count.

We bring the mixers, garnish, ice and glassware, then batch your cocktails on site before service starts.

Every Cocktail Bar booking includes 1 signature cocktail, and weddings include 2. Extra signature drinks can be added to any package.
