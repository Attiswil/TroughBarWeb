Sample menu – tailored to every event.

These drinks are a starting point. We build your menu around your event and what you like to drink.

Pre-batched cocktails

  • Espresso Martini

    Vodka, coffee liqueur and cold-brew espresso, shaken hard.

  • Classic Margarita

    Tequila, orange liqueur and fresh lime, with a salt rim.

  • Aperol Spritz

    Aperol, prosecco and soda with a slice of orange.

  • Passionfruit Mojito

    White rum, passionfruit, lime and fresh mint, topped with soda.

  • Old Fashioned

    Bourbon, bitters and sugar, stirred over a large ice block.

  • Mango Smash

    Our Top End signature. Vodka, ripe mango, lime and mint over crushed ice.

Dirty sodas

  • The Stable Hand

    Cola, coconut syrup, fresh lime and a splash of cream.

  • Dusty Peach

    Lemonade, peach syrup and vanilla cream.

  • Top End Sunset

    Orange soft drink, mango and passionfruit with a cream float.

  • Wet Season

    Lemon-lime soft drink, blue raspberry and coconut cream.

  • Sweet Filly

    Creaming soda, strawberry purée and vanilla cream.

  • Midnight Mare

    Cola, black cherry and vanilla cream, with a cherry on top.

All dirty sodas are alcohol-free.

Who supplies what

On dry hire the client supplies the spirits. We provide a shopping list with quantities.

We bring the mixers, garnish, ice and glassware, and batch the cocktails on site before service. Every Cocktail Bar booking includes 1 signature cocktail, and weddings include 2.
